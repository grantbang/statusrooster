Questions? Answers.

What counts as a monitor?

Each URL you want to check = one monitor. We check it every 60 seconds and alert you if anything goes wrong.

What types of checks do you do?

We do HTTP/HTTPS checks — we load your URL, verify the status code, measure response time, check SSL certificates, and optionally look for a keyword on the page. If anything fails, you get alerted immediately.

Do I need a credit card to start?

Nope. The free plan is genuinely free — 50 monitors with 60-second checks, SSL monitoring, keyword checks, and email + Slack alerts. No trial, no expiration.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes, cancel anytime from your dashboard. You'll keep Pro features through the end of your billing period, then drop back to the free plan. No questions asked.

What about status pages?

Every monitor can have a public status page with 90 days of uptime history and incident timeline. Share it with your users or clients. Pro users get an aggregate page showing all monitors at once.

What makes StatusRooster different?

We're intentionally simple. No 47 features you'll never use. No confusing 4-tier pricing. Just reliable uptime monitoring with a generous free plan and one affordable upgrade. Set up in 30 seconds, not 30 minutes.
