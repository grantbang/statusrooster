--- FIELD: Campaign description ---

StatusRooster (https://statusrooster.com) is an uptime monitoring service. This campaign sends transactional SMS alerts to users who have explicitly opted in to receive text message notifications when their monitored websites or APIs go down or recover. SMS is available on all plans (Free and Pro). Messages are strictly transactional. No marketing, promotional, or advertising content is ever sent.

--- FIELD: How do end-users consent to receive messages? (40-2048 characters) ---

COMPLETE OPT-IN DOCUMENTATION WITH SCREENSHOTS: https://statusrooster.com/sms-consent

Our opt-in flow is inside a logged-in web application, so it cannot be accessed by reviewers directly. The page above is a publicly accessible document with exact reproductions of every screen in the consent flow, the full consent disclosure text, sample messages, and all compliance details.

OPT-IN PROCESS (6 steps):
1. User logs in to StatusRooster and navigates to the Add Monitor or Edit Monitor form.
2. In the Notifications section, user toggles SMS from OFF to ON. SMS is off by default and cannot be pre-selected. Available on all plans (Free and Pro).
3. User enters their phone number in E.164 format (e.g., +15551234567).
4. An inline consent disclosure is displayed directly below the phone input. It reads: "By enabling SMS alerts, you consent to receive transactional text messages about monitor status (down/recovery). Msg frequency varies by monitor activity. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help." with links to our Privacy Policy, Terms, and SMS Consent page.
5. User clicks "Create Monitor" or "Save Changes" to submit.
6. A confirmation SMS is sent immediately: "StatusRooster: SMS alerts enabled. You'll receive texts when your monitors go down or recover. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Terms: https://statusrooster.com/terms"

No SMS is sent until all 6 steps are complete. Users opt out by replying STOP or removing their phone number in-app. Phone numbers are not shared with third parties.
